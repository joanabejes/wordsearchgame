# AquaSearch
It is an educational word search game that teaches ecology an environmental awareness through play - designed for playes of all ages.

Every hidden word was chosen to introduce players important ecological themes: sustainable solutions, environmental protection, and how knowledge helps prevent bigger problems in the future.

## The Educational Purpose Behind
- Introduce ecological vocabulary in a playful way
- Raise awareness about sustainability and water conservation
- Show that environmental education can start with something as simple as a game
- Support a better future through knowledge and prevention

## How to Play AquaSearch
1. Open the game in your browser
2. Look at the word list or hints below the board
3. Click the **first letter** of a word, and then click the **last letter**
4. If the path is correct, the word turns green and is crossed of the list
5. Find all the words to save the ecosystem
